Customer Segments by RFM

Each customer becomes three numbers: days since their last purchase (recency), how many purchases (frequency) and total spend (monetary). K-Means groups customers with similar numbers. With 4 groups the project's 2,000 customers split into VIP Champions, Loyal, New and At Risk, and the groups explain 93.9% of the variance.

  1. Move the slider to try 2 to 8 clusters and watch the elbow chart: each extra cluster explains less.
  2. Read each segment's size and average recency, purchases and spend.
  3. Switch to Your own transactions and load a CSV, or try the 60-customer sample.
