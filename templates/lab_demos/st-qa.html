Member Q&A by Meaning

Ask about a concierge client in plain words ("Where does Sophia want to fly by private jet?"). The engine finds the member, ranks that member's messages by meaning rather than shared words (sentence embeddings from all-MiniLM-L6-v2), and reads the answer out of the best one. On 26 labelled, reworded questions it found the right message 25 times (96%).

  1. Pick a labelled question or type your own about one of the 10 members, or switch to Your own text and search lines you paste.
  2. Press Ask. The first time downloads the language model and its runtime (about 27 MB).
  3. Read the answer, then the member's messages ranked by similarity.
