ASL Word Signs: Learn, Practise, Get Checked

Learn one of 29 STEM and computing signs (computer, email, science, math...) at your own speed, then sign it. While you sign, every new frame is compared with how the sign is made in the training clips, and when it stops matching, the frame where it went wrong is flagged, with what it looks like instead. It does not catch every mistake; how often it does is measured under the check. When you finish, the trained model gives its best match and its score for all 29 signs. On 58 held-out clips the model's top guess was right 75.86% of the time and in its top three 94.83% of the time (chance is 3.45%).

  1. Choose a sign and watch it slowly: play it at a quarter speed, pause, or step frame by frame.
  2. Check an attempt: sign it to your camera, or try a held-out clip or a made-up mistake (a clip that starts as your sign and switches to another half-way).
  3. Watch the timeline fill in as the frames arrive. It turns red at the frame where the attempt went wrong; the final scores appear when it ends.
