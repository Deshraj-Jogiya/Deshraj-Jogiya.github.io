Fraud Rule Tester

Three simple rules screen 345,524 simulated card transactions, 2,591 of them planted fraud. Loosen or tighten each rule and see the trade-off a fraud team lives with: catching more fraud means more false alarms on real customers.

  1. Load the simulated transactions (about 3.8 MB, once), or switch to Your own transactions and paste or upload a CSV.
  2. Move the sliders: how many charges in how many minutes count as card testing, and how large a purchase counts as a spike.
  3. Watch precision (share of alerts that are fraud) and recall (share of fraud caught) move.
Data

One row per charge: customer, time, amount, then optionally foreign (1 or 0, or a location; blank, 0, no or domestic count as home) and fraud (1 or 0, only to score the rules). Time is a date and time (2026-03-01 14:05) or a number of seconds. Without a fraud column you see the alerts but not precision or recall. The example below is made up to show each rule once; replace it with an export from a bank or card statement. It is read in your browser and never uploaded.

From FinTech Credit Risk & Fraud Command Center (FINDINGS). The transactions are simulated with a fixed seed and the fraud is planted by the simulator; the rules never read the labels. The rules are etl/fraud_rules.py rewritten for the browser; every deploy checks them against the Python at five settings, and through the CSV upload path, and they must agree on every count.
