Airflow DAG Backfill & Retry Replay

A pipeline runs three steps every day: create the data, transform it with PySpark, then check it. Here it catches up on five missed days at once (a backfill), and some steps fail along the way.

  1. Press Run the 5 days. Two failures are already set up.
  2. Red squares failed; orange ones never ran because an earlier step failed. Tap a square to read its log.
  3. Press Fix it and re-run on a failed square, or add retries and run again.
Set up your own failures

    The three steps are the real tasks of the Job Market Lakehouse Airflow DAG. The real DAG runs on demand; this replay puts it on a daily schedule so retries and backfills have something to show.
